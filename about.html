OUR STORY

Fragrance meets emotion.

At S369, we believe a fragrance should be more than just a pleasant scent — it should be an unforgettable experience. Although we are a new startup, our journey began long before our launch. Over the last one and a half years, we have passionately invested in research and development to create perfumes that deliver exceptional longevity, powerful projection and a luxurious experience with every spray.

S369 was created with one simple belief: premium fragrance should not have to come with an unreasonable price tag.

Our mission is to reach every perfume lover and provide them with a scent they can trust, enjoy and feel emotionally connected to. We understand the joy and confidence a great fragrance brings, and we work tirelessly to ensure every spray leaves a lasting impression — not just in the air, but in the heart.
